# Source Boundary

**In scope:**
- A-share fundamental factors from financial statements
- Value, quality, growth, composite factors
- IC analysis, grouped returns, Fama-MacBeth
- Pandadata APIs: `get_fina_performance`, `get_fina_reports`, `get_factor`, `get_market_data`
- Cross-sectional z-score standardization
- TTM accumulation of quarterly data
- Factor decay analysis
- Sub-sample stability testing

**Out of scope:**
- OHLCV/price-based alpha factors (covered by other QUANTSKILLS)
- Stock screening/filtering (covered by `skill-stock-screener`)
- Index valuation/PE percentile (covered by `skill-index-valuation-rotation`)
- Stock due diligence reports (covered by `skill-a-share-stock-dossier`)
- Machine learning on factors (future skill)
- Portfolio optimization (covered by `skill-portfolio-construction`)
- Real-time fundamental factor trading
- Non-China markets
- Alternative data (sentiment, search trends, supply chain)
